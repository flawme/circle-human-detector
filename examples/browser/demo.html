    How this works

    The engine scores movement dynamics, not geometry: how angular velocity varies, how drawing speed modulates around the circle, tremor structure, start/end ramps, and timestamp irregularity. A mathematically perfect circle with human dynamics scores HUMAN_LIKE; a sloppy circle with machine dynamics scores SUSPICIOUS.

    INCONCLUSIVE means the engine refuses to guess: the stroke was too short, not circle-like, or the evidence was ambiguous. SUSPICIOUS is a probabilistic signal, never proof — use it to trigger a fallback challenge, not to block an account.
